A starting point for fire protection takeoff.

1. Pick the right example.

Mark the legend as an exclusion. Select a symbol on this page and label it using the code from your drawing. The suggested codes are editable examples.

2. Check the installation count.

Scan matching shapes, inspect numbered hits, reject false positives and add missed items manually. The same engine runs for every trade; these pages do not add trained trade detectors.

3. Apply your rates.

Enter material and labour rates per counted item. Export evidence, a quantity/price CSV and a marked PDF. Missing rates and unresolved items stay visible.

A useful review question

Which device type and scope are we counting?

Treat every device code as a distinct item and verify it against the drawing legend. Quantity review does not establish coverage, compliance or system performance.

What this workspace covers

Counting declared symbols on one page at a time, reviewing their evidence and pricing supported quantities. Digital PDFs are the primary workflow; raster matching can miss items.

Outside the current scope

Coverage assessment, code compliance, pipe and cable lengths, hydraulic calculations and system certification.

Tax, markup, accessories and other job costs are excluded. No live supplier feeds or automatic technical interpretation.
