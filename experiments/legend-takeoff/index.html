The idea

The drawing already
has a dictionary.
Let’s put it to work.

Before an electrical job can be priced, someone has to count what’s on the plans. Different drawings use different symbols, and small mistakes can travel straight into an estimate.

Legend starts with the symbol examples printed on the drawing itself. The goal: less repetitive counting, with a clear path from each quantity back to its evidence.

One connected workflow

Count. Check. Correct. Price.

Keep the reviewer in the loop, from the first symbol to the final assumption.

01 / FIND THE PATTERN

Count from the legend.

Mark the legend as an exclusion area, label an example symbol, then scan that page for matching equipment.

Example → installed instances
02 / FOLLOW THE EVIDENCE

See what was counted.

Select a quantity to inspect numbered hits. Legend examples are excluded, and every detection keeps its page and location.

Quantity → visible evidence
03 / HANDLE EXCEPTIONS

Make uncertainty visible.

Confirm, flag or reject individual detections. Review decisions stay separate from the original results and quantities recalculate.

Review → updated quantity
04 / APPLY YOUR RATES

Build a price draft.

Enter material and labour rates per unit. See priced subtotals, identify gaps, and export the assumptions with your quantities.

Quantity × rate → draft
Make the next step easier

A clearer count.
A quicker price draft.

Bring your own rates. The worksheet carries reviewed supported quantities into material and labour subtotals, so a correction flows through to the price.

Missing rates stay unpriced. Unresolved items stay visible. You can see what the draft covers before taking it further.

Built for a hackathon

A working experiment.
With visible limits.

We’re testing the workflow before making an accuracy promise. The useful demo includes the cases where it struggles.

  • Works todayOpen your own PDF, select an example, scan one page at a time, review the evidence and apply your rates. A six-sheet synthetic demo is also available.
  • Honest outputsExport current-page evidence JSON, quantities and pricing CSV, plus a PDF marked with your reviewed hits. The separate fixture demo retains its original outputs.
  • Still to proveBroad accuracy on real electrical drawings, independent human labels and automatic legend interpretation. Raster matching can miss equipment. AI review is not implemented; no cloud AI calls run in this pilot.
